How the forecast is learned: the average of the last two weeks, each weekday at its usual share of the week, and the trend of the last eight weeks at half strength (at most ±5% a week). "How reliable" is that model refit as of each of the last four weeks and scored on the week after, which it had not seen. Learned from planned units where the data has them; from units done otherwise, which under-reads days the floor was short. Planned rate defaults to the plan the data carries (FCLM's Process Path Rollup).
