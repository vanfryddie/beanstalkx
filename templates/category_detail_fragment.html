How this score works

Every cross-training record (one per associate + process) earns points toward 100 based on its proficiency state: Proficient = 100 (the goal — fully qualified), Practice = 60 (at least 20 hours within the last 90 days, actively current), Refresh = 30 (at least 20 hours somewhere in the last 180 days, but coming due for renewal), Lapsed = 0 (fewer than 20 hours anywhere in the last 180 days). The score is the average points per record.
